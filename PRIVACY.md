# Privacy

Your data stays with you. Free Workout Timer doesn't collect anything about how you use it.

- Your timers are saved on your phone and never sent to us.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- The app only goes online for things that work for you, never for us. Today that is two things:
  - **App updates.** An automatic check for app updates. It carries no information about you.
  - **Class mode (beta), only while you're in a class.** The instructor's pause and skip pass between the phones in the class, end-to-end encrypted, through free public relays we don't run and can't read. The relays see scrambled text under a random label, never your workout or anything about you; like any server, they can see that a phone connected. A workout only leaves your phone inside a class code you choose to show; nobody in the middle sees it.
- **Camera, only for class mode.** When you choose to join a class, the camera reads the class code. Nothing the camera sees is saved or sent.
- The only system-level prompts that ever appear are the camera request (only when you join a class), notifications for the on-screen timer, and the standard App Store / Play Store review request.

If that ever stops being true, this file will say so.
